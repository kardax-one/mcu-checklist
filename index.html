About this timeline & image credits

Ordering follows Digital Spy’s MCU watch guide (November 11, 2026). Chronological order includes its episode splits; marking an entire season in release order marks every part. Slingshot appears in chronology and is also included in release order. The guide excludes X-Men ’97 and Your Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man. Years on cards are release years, including in chronological view. Phase assignments follow the MCU Wiki. Older Marvel Television shows, Eyes of Wakanda and Marvel Zombies have no numbered phase in that source and appear in “Outside the phases.” Group totals count all stories, regardless of active filters. The Doctor Doom background is artwork from the MCU Wiki. This is an independent fan checklist; Marvel and poster artwork belong to their respective owners. Posters are loaded from external image sources and require internet access.
